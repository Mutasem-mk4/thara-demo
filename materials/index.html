Scope

A fictional open-field vegetable case uses real 2015–2024 NASA POWER weather and generic FAO-56 coefficients. The percentage compares entered turn depth with estimated crop use. Effective rainfall, soil-water storage and measured field losses are outside the balance. It is not a forecast, an irrigation prescription or a validated best-crop selection.

No farmer interviews, measured farm, water savings or field impact are claimed. Challenge resource eligibility and the local cutoff remain unverified. The team reviews and submits the project itself.
