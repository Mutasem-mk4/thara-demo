# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An Arabic-speaking vegetable grower in the Middle Jordan Valley, planning a field season alone on a phone in daylight. Judges need an equally usable English view. The ready example represents a fictional field and supply schedule.

## Product Purpose

Compare up to three crop and planting-date scenarios against recurring water turns. Ten years of NASA temperatures drive a disclosed Hargreaves and generic Kc estimate. Success is a clear comparison with an experiment, auditable source and printable plan. Agronomic adequacy and field impact require further validation.

## Brand Personality

Clear, grounded, calm. A working field notebook with precise evidence and approachable Arabic.

## Anti-references

Avoid sales dashboards, fabricated savings, traffic-light suitability scores, complex maps, oversized marketing statistics, and AI chat interfaces. The primary task should be accessible immediately.

## Design Principles

1. Facts, assumptions, and unknowns remain visibly distinct.
2. A source and a calculation trail accompany every influential result.
3. Crop, date and water edits affect the relevant modeled values transparently.
4. Real historical weather is kept separate from the fictional field.
5. A grower can complete the journey without an adviser. Put optional methodological detail behind disclosures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable Arabic, RTL with isolated dates and units, keyboard access, visible focus, text labels for states, mobile layouts, and reduced-motion support. WCAG AA contrast is a design target, not a certified conformance claim.
