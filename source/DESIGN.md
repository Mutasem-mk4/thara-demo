# THARA interface

An Arabic-speaking grower uses a phone outdoors in daylight, without an adviser. The approved rebuild uses a white surface, deep green ink and controls, blue water marks, and ochre/red-brown comparison states accompanied by words. IBM Plex Sans Arabic is bundled locally. Controls are at least 48px high. The THARA mark from Claude's initial rebuild is retained.

The journey moves through field, water turns, crop options and results. A labelled fictional Deir Alla example skips entry using saved authentic historical data. New own-field input begins blank. Coordinates and maps are optional alternatives to an area reference point.

The result first shows an unsorted comparison of up to three crop/date options. The selected option shows its peak complete turn interval and the turn-to-use ratio. Water and date experiments appear next, followed by the weekly chart. Weekly totals supplement the complete-interval calculation. Plant-family, heat and rain details are disclosed progressively. Methods expose profile sources, assumptions, coverage, a daily trace and NASA provenance.

Arabic follows RTL with isolated numbers and units. The season axis remains chronological from left to right in either language. Weekly buttons also have a select control for touch and keyboard users. Status labels carry meaning in words. Motion is short and respects reduced motion.

The browser print view includes all crop options and expands methodological summaries. Dense daily traces stay available in the application and downloaded audit. Results use screening language because soil storage, effective rain, local crop calibration and measured irrigation performance require further evidence.
